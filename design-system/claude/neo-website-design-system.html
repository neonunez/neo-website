Typography

Two families with intent: Inter for all prose and UI, JetBrains Mono for the engineered voice — keycaps, token counts, paths, and prompts. The neo logo is drawn with geometric SVG paths, not a font. The scale is compact and label-dense, reflecting the terminal/dossier feel.
